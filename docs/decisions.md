| P26    | Confidence per field                      | Each field of each footballer carries a confidence level (high, medium or low) with the sources that agree. Decided by Jalel on 4 October 2026 after a cross-check of 30 footballers (115 high, 25 medium, 10 low; caps are the weak field) |

| P34    | A footballer with no Tunisia row          | His 0 caps is rated medium when at least two sources agree that he is not an international (the English and French pages both show no Tunisia row, or one page and Wikidata listing no national team) and no row was skipped; low when only one source says so; never high. | P39    | One page against Wikidata on a birth date | The page wins, rated low and flagged until Jalel confirms it: the same rule as for the position line. Decided on 4 October 2026                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
