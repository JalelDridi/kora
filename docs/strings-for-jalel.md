# Strings for Jalel: Sprint 2, part 2b (Chkoun?, Sources, privacy)

Every key added by the game page, the result, the Sources page, the privacy draft, the footer and the hub card, with what it means in English, the French draft, and the two Derja values **as shipped**. Nothing here was checked by a Derja speaker.

- **DRAFT**: drafted by Claude (an AI), modelled on the existing Derja strings, not run through a translation tool and with no Modern Standard Arabic on purpose, but not checked by a Derja speaker. They may ship for the soft launch (P13); Jalel rewrites or approves each one.
- **French placeholder (needs Jalel)**: until 7 October 2026 the long texts of the Sources and privacy pages carried the French draft in all three files. Under P51 they are now Derja DRAFTS (Arabic script and Arabizi), written by Claude on 7 October 2026 from the French; the legal-ish privacy wording in particular is his to check.
- **Country names**: written on 6 October 2026 from Wikidata's Tunisian-Arabic (`aeb`) labels, as Jalel asked ("you can search for them surely"); he corrects any he dislikes. The doubtful ones are marked in the last table.

Where to edit: `messages/ar-TN.json`, `messages/ar-Latn-TN.json`, `messages/fr.json` (and the two country files). Keep `{n}`, `{name}`, `{time}`, `{country}`, `{author}`, `{licence}` as they are; Western digits only; the tests check every key exists in every locale.

Open questions in the strings:

- `chkoun.share.header`: is it the same as `games.chkoun.name`? Drafted as `Kora · ` + the game name (the share text starts with it).
- `chkoun.tiles.country` is the country of the guessed footballer's **current club** (D-S2-7 compares clubs); drafted as "Pays du club". `chkoun.tiles.governorate` covers a birthplace abroad too, so it is drafted as "Né à" (born in).
- `footer.privacy` in Arabic script: الخصوصية is close to standard Arabic; he may prefer another word.
- `chkoun.caps.band0` to `band4` are digits and the same in every locale.

- `privacy.analytics.body` changed (22 Oct 2026): it now lists the six 30–0 events and what they carry (D-S3-13) beside Chkoun?'s five, in French and both Derja files; the new values are in the "Sources and privacy pages" table. - `season.result.record`: should "30-0-0" read wins-draws-losses ({w}-{d}-{l}, as shipped, also the share text's second line) or only wins-losses like "82-0"? The game's name, 30–0, reads W-L; a season can have draws, so W-L alone would need the draws said elsewhere.

- `season.rating`, `season.result.teamRating`, `season.hall.rating`, `season.replay.note`: تقييم / Ta9yim and بالتقييمات / bel ta9yimet are standard-Arabic (MSA) nominal forms; the spoken form is نوتة / nota (plural نوتات / notet). The share text's third line uses `season.rating`, so change them together.
- Goals: `season.inputs.goalsPerCap` and `season.result.goals` say أهداف / Ahdef, the standard word; spoken Tunisian says قول / goal (plural قوال, "goulet"). `season.result.goals` is الأهداف (لها-عليها) / Ahdef (leha-3liha) for "goals for-against".

- `season.inputs.titlesValue`: إفريقيا / Ifrikia stands for the CAF club competitions (Champions League and Confederation Cup together).

- `season.rules` says "up to five" (خمسة ولا أقل / khamsa wala a9al / cinq au plus): a spin can offer fewer than five candidates (a widened spin often offers 1 or 2).
- `season.result.home` / `away`: في دارها / برّا (Fi darha / Barra). Commentators also say على ميدانها for home.
- `season.result.win|draw|loss`: ربح / تعادل / خسارة (Rbe7 / T3adel / Khsara) mix verbs and nouns; spoken alternatives are ربحنا / تعادلنا / خسرنا. تعادل / T3adel is also MSA; spoken نول / nul (from French) or ماتش نول.
- `season.result.round`: الجولة / Jawla is MSA; sports talk says الجورني / el journée.
- `season.hall.record`: النتيجة / El natija is MSA; spoken candidates السكور / es-score, or the bare record with no label.
- `season.result.points`, `season.hall.points`: النقاط / No9at is MSA; spoken النقط / en-no9ot.
- `season.share.points`: نقطة / no9ta after the number (Arabic takes the singular after 11 and more; with 3 to 10 points it would read نقاط). A season always has 0 to 90 points, so the singular is wrong only for 3 to 10.

- `season.board.off` and `season.board.notCounted`: الترتيب / el tartib; spoken alternative الكلاسمون / el classement.
