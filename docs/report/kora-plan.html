The mechanic that spreads

Same puzzle for everyone, once a day, with an emoji result you paste anywhere. Wordle, Who Are Ya?, NBA 82-0's Hoople all grew on it. The share grid is the product.

What we don't compete with

Tunisian Football Fantasy already exists and is heavier. Transport and pharmacy apps exist. Free football APIs don't cover Ligue 1 Tunisie; Wikidata does cover Tunisian players.

What a recruiter will see

Seven places the engineering shows

1 · Data pipeline with provenance

Nightly SPARQL, normalisation, overrides, a diff opened as a PR, audience reports feeding a review queue.

2 · Server-authoritative engine

Pure functions, property tests, a deterministic schedule that is correct across time zones and never repeats a player within 90 days.

3 · Redis the right way

Sorted-set leaderboards, per-id and per-IP limits, one result per player per day (idempotency, again).

4 · Arabic and French in one layout

RTL, Arabic numerals and dates, two scripts for every name, a native review before launch.

5 · Performance on a 3G Android

Static page with ISR, edge share images, a Lighthouse budget enforced in CI, PWA offline for today's puzzle.

6 · Measured growth

Funnels from open to share, D1/D7 retention by language, decisions taken from the numbers and written down.

7 · Abuse without accounts

Anonymous ids, rate limits, the answer never leaves the server until the day ends; a public write-up if it is ever cheated.

Launch plan

Day 0: you post the share grid of puzzle #1 in the two or three biggest fan groups and on a Facebook page for the game (D7). Derby days get a pinned puzzle and a post. The game itself never posts; people do, because the grid is theirs.

Plan

Twelve working days to a live, measured game

Days 1–3

Foundation

Repo, CI, engine with property tests, data pipeline and first pool, schedule generator.

Days 4–7

The game

Page in AR and FR, server-side guessing, share grid, streaks, PWA, Playwright, Lighthouse budget, PostHog, Sentry, domain. Launch.

Days 8–12

Keep them

Leaderboards on Redis, optional sign-in, OG result images, report button and review queue, first nightly data PR.

Week 3+

Grow

Second mode, derby pins, page content, retention work from the numbers, portfolio case study with real figures.

Risks

Stale clubs → overrides and reports. Facebook browser quirks → test in it. Nobody shares → measure share rate from day one and iterate. Photo licences → CC only, credited.

Costs

Zero. Vercel Hobby, Neon, Upstash, PostHog free tiers; Sentry and the domain from the Student Pack. No ads or prizes (Hobby terms).
