Game 2 in detail: the growth bet

30–0: draft legends into a Ligue 1 club and chase a perfect season

1 · Spin

A club (Espérance, Club Africain, Étoile, CSS, Stade Tunisien, …) and an era (80s, 90s, 2000s, 2010s, now). Both random, both re-spinnable once.

2 · Draft

Eleven positions; for each, three legends from that club's history across eras, with computed ratings 60–99 and the inputs shown (caps, goals, honours, era).

3 · Simulate and share

Thirty matches against the league, seeded and reproducible. The record and the squad card go to "Today's top sides" and to Facebook as an image.

Why it spreads

Every Tunisian fan has an opinion on the all-time Espérance XI. The card invites the argument; the argument brings the next player. 82-0 went viral on exactly this.

Engineering inside

A documented rating formula, a deterministic season engine with property tests (same seed, same season; a 99-rated XI wins more than a 70 one, always), an edge-generated share image, a live board over SSE.

Language

Derja first, in both scripts people actually type

شكون اللاعب؟

Derja, Arabic script

How fan pages post. 34% of comments.

Chkoun el la3eb?

Derja, Arabizi

How people type on phones. 53% of comments. Recognised by Ethnologue as Tunisia's informal script.

C'est qui ?

French

Second language of the web in Tunisia; the diaspora.

Rules

No Modern Standard Arabic in the UI. Copy written in Derja by you, reviewed by one more Tunisian before launch; no machine translation. Western digits everywhere. Player names in the chosen script with the Latin spelling underneath.

Search that understands Derja

A transliteration table (3→ع, 7→ح, 9→ق, kh/5→خ) plus fuzzy matching, tested against 200 real misspellings collected from fan pages. Not an LLM: fast, free, predictable.

Sources: study of 1.2M Tunisian social-media comments (arXiv 2020); Ethnologue 2016.

Why not a chatbot

It would cost money at any real traffic and add nothing to the share loop. These four are bounded, cached, measurable (hint opens, report shares) and fail to templates.

Free tiers, verified

Gemini Flash: free, about 1,500 requests/day. Groq: 1,000/day. Cloudflare Workers AI: 10,000 neurons/day, second provider if Gemini's quota is hit.

What a recruiter will see

Seven places the engineering shows

1 · Data pipeline with provenance

Nightly SPARQL, overrides, audience reports, an AI that proposes and a human that approves, every change a PR.

2 · Deterministic engines

Seeded season simulator, time-zone-correct schedule, grid rarity computed from everyone's answers; property-tested.

3 · Three transports by need

ISR + timer, SSE on Redis, WebSockets in Durable Objects, with the written reasons.

4 · A dialect in two scripts

RTL and LTR in one layout, transliteration-aware search, no machine translation.

5 · AI with a ceiling

Cached, quota-aware, template fallbacks, measured by its own events.

6 · Fast on a 3G Android

Lighthouse budget in CI, edge share images, PWA offline for today's puzzles.

7 · Abuse without accounts

Anonymous ids, per-id and per-IP limits, answers that never leave the server, one result per player per game per day (the idempotency habit from Payout Ledger), and a public write-up if the game is ever cheated.
